Annotations

  1. Last nightweed-vape hogging

    Contributor: FrerkDaddy420

    The author might have been imbibing in cannabis, which is legal in California.

    hogging refers to keeping the vape for too long, more commonly referred to as bogarting

  2. grogging

    Contributor: xxxfrerkman69

    Grogging is the practice of extracting residual alcohol soaked into the wooden staves of empty spirit or whiskey casks by filling or soaking them with water. Here, this is meant metaphorically: the narrator 'extracts' alcohol from the winner's lounge.

  3. eggnogging

    Contributor: xX_Fr3rk_Xx

    Eggnog and it's verb eggnogging is a synecdoche for alcohol in general.

  4. Now morning blogging /
    Pain in the noggin

    Contributor: Dr_Frerk_MD

    The narrator appears to be slightly hungover and suffering from dolor capitis (literally "pain of the head")

  5. Must be embogging.

    Contributor: Frerkspeare

    from “to embog,” an archaic verb that means to sink something into a bog, or to get all stuck or tangled up as if in one... it's like... it’s pretty much just basically just an old-fashioned way of saying “bogging down.”

    To use this in an example sentence: “The army was embogged in the marshes,” or figuratively, “The FDA got embogged in endless debate over whether retatutide is a biologic and so millions have to die.”

    Here the author needs to get his noggin (head) down and start writing something.

  6. Must be backlogging

    Contributor: frerk_from_accounting

    The narrator seems to be under pressure to create a backlog of blog post to be under less pressure, to depressurize so to say.

  7. progging

    Contributor: frerks_mom

    to prog means to to search, prowl, or forage about—traditionally for food, supplies, or items washed up on beaches or on riverbeds, but here again the author is ingeniously using it as a _metaphor_ find ideas for blog posts or stuff that fits in the complex rhyme schema.

  8. Brain forest logging

    Contributor: frerk_touches_grass

    Here the narrator likes the creative process to the destruction of the amazon.

    In Nobel Laureate Graham Greene's 1954 short story "The Destructors" where a London gang systematically dismantles an elderly man's house from the inside there is a quote ""Destruction after all is a form of creation."

  10. Captain’s logging?

    Contributor: CaptainFrerk

LaForge realigns Data, but they miss the talks, so Picard plans time travel to stop Schmidhuber. https://frerk.co/trek

  11. “Pascal’s mogging”?

    Contributor: Blaise_Frerkscal

    The inspiration for the piece a portmanteau of pascal's mugging a thought experiment and mogging, which is youth slang for showing off.

    Combined this is satire of the common thing done in ea where you 'mog' people by working on something with low probability but high ev events.

  12. hotdogging

    Contributor: definitely_not_frerk

    THIS IS NOT MEANT IN A SEXUAL WAY. It means: "the act of one who hot-dogs; the performance of intricate, daring, or flamboyant stunts."

  13. It’s a dog an’
    Pony show

    Contributor: FrerkFacts

    In an interview with Playboy, Frerk gives advice to aspiring artists: "If you're going to do it, just fucking do it. Say something and quit this bullshit and say something that matters. If you stay in one place for too long, it will just kill your ability to make anything useful to anyone.

    As an artist, you must blow yourself up to move on. You have to destroy something to make anything that is real and that speaks to the human experience in a meaningful way."
