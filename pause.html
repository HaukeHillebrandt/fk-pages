Pause

“You open the heavy brass key box your grandfather left you, only to find a perfectly functioning remote control that allows you to pause the world for everyone except the person holding it…”

I'm fucking freaking out. How does this thing work? I press the pause button and everything around me is paused. Like... What does it pause? All other life forms? All other moving things? Are people dying around the world because I paused the world and the machines are still working? This is a very dangerous technology. I must not press pause.

But I could also use it for good I guess. I must start experimenting. I must acquire resources.

This is complicated. This is a lot of responsibility. What can I do? There's the gimmicky stuff, that I should probably not do, like act as if I can teleport myself, by just pausing everything, and then going somewhere.

Hold on. Have I maybe just lost it? That's the most likely explanation surely. There's no such thing as a remote that can pause things. Though it did seem to work. Or is this an elaborate prank? I'm hesitant to pause again, because I don't know what the consequences are. This is such a powerful technology. Like, many people could die. I could die, who knows maybe I could pause myself. But it does seem to work.

Let's back up a little: when I tried the first time, things just became really silent around me. And this city is anything but silent. It was disorienting. I look out of the window. Crowds of people frozen - I get an immediate prediction error. It’s eerie. I'm panicking. I hit the button again, and then, people start moving again.

It might be an intentional binding effect. When you cause something to happen your perception is such that the action you perform and its consequences appear closer together in perceptual time than in reality. You can do fun psychology experiments where people believe that the light flicker they cause by pressing a button appears 'before' they actually press the button. Maybe it’s something like that? And all the people outside are actors? Is this some sort of television show? Seems hard to coordinate. I don't like to indulge in conspiracy theories. I believe in science but this is scary.

I don't hear any sirens. That suggests that everything must have been paused and not only humans (or all biological life?). Otherwise there would have been car crashes that’s for sure. Or general disorder.

The most likely cause is that I've lost it and am suffering from some sort of schizophrenia because things cannot just be paused. You cannot just pause things. I mean... of course there's relativity and all that and the physics etc.

It's just... I feel fine. I feel like I'm reasoning perfectly normally. My vision is fine. Of course, my heart rate is up because this is crazy. Should I talk to anyone about it? That would be very dangerous.

I must hide this dangerous weapon, so that others can't get it. Or maybe destroy it? Perhaps I should destroy it. That is to say, I ought to destroy it. Nobody should be able to pause the world. It’s too much power. What if there are others though who have similar remotes and could as well?

Hold on, I’m probably just crazy. Why would my grandfather have been in the possession of an experimental weapon like this… like a temporal weapon? And it looks like a TV remote. Maybe someone roofied me? But then… I haven’t left the house all day. Perhaps there was something on the remote. Transdermal LSD or something. Maybe I’ve been poisoned. I should induce vomiting. It would be unpleasant but prudent. Hold on.

Alright. This didn’t work. Only dry heaving, my stomach is empty. Besides, does that even do anything if I was drugged transdermally? I’m not thinking clearly, I must remain cool. I need to wash my hands, yes, if I was roofied transdermally, there might be residue.

Okay, done.

Remote is still there staring at me. I won’t touch it without gloves. Or maybe it’s emitting some sort of airborne roofie substance, I must contain it. Bag it. I’ll open the window. I’ll put it in a clear sealed bag while not touching it, then put it away. Good, done.

I don’t feel very different though. Can I do complicated reasoning? Maybe I should do some math. Maybe I just lost it. Unlikely, I’m 40, not a common age for becoming delusional. Maybe I should still see a psychiatrist or neurologist. I should see a neurologist. Make up some vague symptoms, headaches and mild visual distortions and hallucinations in my periphery, I’ll ask an LLM about it. Don’t want to be committed. “This guy believes he can pause the world using a remote.” I’ll just check out if they find something unusual. I need an MRI. This is craaazy. You can’t just pause.
