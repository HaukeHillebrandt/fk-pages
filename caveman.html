Musings of a caveman

We’ve been tracking this fucking herd for like two days. This is literally insane. I want meat. My mind is racing. Tonc seems skinny and pale and not gonna have another day’s march in him. He’s much younger than me and exceptionally skilled with the bow but lacks the fat reserves required to march for another day. He’ll probably be dead by the end of the day and he knows it. Smart kid. I’ve been reflecting on what I’ve learned over the years and have been trying to impart to him the most important learnings in these past days, because I’m much older, but maybe that’s love’s labours lost, there’s no way that we can track the herd for two more days, and they do seem tired, but not nearly tired enough for any of them to collapse today. And you need three guys at least to corner them for this size of herd. Persistence hunting requires a lot of patience, let me tell you. It really does. To be honest, I was never very good at it. Not patient enough. Maybe the reason I got so old is that I always get lucky it seems. Maybe it’s all about the gods being kind to me.

The truth is the gods always sent me cliffs to drive the animals over at just the right time, and then I come back boastfully as if it was my own doing, but it mostly is just getting lucky with the gods sending you cliffs to drive the animals over. I actually did have this method for years, where I just stayed around cliffs and would drive the animals over them - I knew that area so well, I knew where all the major cliffs were, and the animals are kind of stupid in that way... corner them, scare them, then right over the cliff these retards jump to their deaths. Then you just pick them up, all fresh meat, no struggle, no danger. It got so insane that at one point I took a bunch of women to the bottom of a pretty good cliff and told them to collect dry wood and berries and stuff and just wait there. And make it rain I did, that very first day. Rain fucking big juicy animals, falling from the sky. They were fucking loving it, all that meat. Looked at me as if I invented fire. Ohh... good times. I think they did carry my children later they do look a bit like me but maybe it was my cousin that sneaky fuck.

But then, like I don’t know if there are only so many animals in the area because either they figure it out that you’re there and don’t go there anymore or you just deplete the stock in the area, because you can’t even eat that many animals at once before they spoil, and then at some point you gotta move on to a different area. You also do want to mix it up and not overdo it in one area but rotate the area so as to not hunt the area dry. Like, those cliff years were some really fat years. The area was so easy to defend too. Very few others around that were trying to come kill you in the first place and those that did, by the time they crossed all those waters and got to where you were, they were usually so skinny and tired out, you can just easily pick them up one by one and kill them. If they travel with women, you take their women, but sometimes the women are already too haggard. I remember when we got here we were pretty tired and skinny too, but there was literally no one around. We were pretty surprised by that. Anyway. Yeah, Tonc, I don’t know, maybe he’ll make it. He’s got talent that’s for sure. I wonder if I should share more of the honey I’ve got left, but I’m not sure if at this stage it’ll just make him sick, the honey is also a bit old. That honey was like so so good a couple days ago. Just chugged it by the fistful and didn’t get stung at all, which made it even sweeter.

If I make it back to camp, I will start sharing more. Like, you need to feed your close friends, if you don’t, then if you don’t get lucky on the hunt or injure your foot and need some food for a few days or something you’re fucking done for, if you didn’t share enough with others before. Obviously, depends on how bad your injury is... if people smell that you won’t make it—and they absolutely can smell it—no one will share with you until you’re back in good health and can give back. Smart folks know that you don’t just need to watch out for getting fat enough yourself for when it gets colder, but you also need to fatten up your friends, and your women, or they won’t make it through the cold. I think that’s the most important thing I’ve learned. But you gotta be careful who you share with, some people won’t reciprocate and just exploit you. Best strategy is to start handing out small portions to everyone liberally, then see who gives back, and ramp up what you give out over time, if they reciprocate. Give the most meat to those who share the most.

Also don’t underestimate or make enemies with women. They might be weak, but they can, and they do, I’ve seen it many times, pick up large stones and bash your head in while you sleep, if they think you wronged them somehow like didn’t share enough meat with them, and then claim you ate all their berries, and you can’t say anything because they’ve got some young hothead under their spell, who’ll go fucking apeshit if you say it’s not true.

Talking about bashing in some heads, I’m FUCKING LIVID about Brok. He’s taking way more than his fair share during the hunts that sneaky fuck, and I suspect him of having a secret stash of honey somewhere, otherwise he wouldn’t be so fucking fat that fucking fat piece of shit. Maybe I should stalk him when I get back, see where all that honey comes from and just arrow him down, and take over all of his nests. No way I can beat that fucking fat piece of shit in a fair fight, he’s way too heavy, and I’m not that young anymore. But he’s so unaware and dumb too, he doesn’t even talk that much. Never liked the guy to be honest, whenever he gets lucky during a hunt and he’s sharing any meat at all, which happens rarely, he always fucking acts as if he’s the alpha and we should all pray to him and put on some fucking ceremony for him.

I wish Taq was still with us. He was such a mensch, always sharing meat liberally, and very smart. He always did what no one else was thinking of. Like finding good water holes when everyone was too focused on hunting, or watching out for wolves. He had such potential, easily could have made it clan leader, if it wasn’t for that wolf tearing him apart.

If I survive this hunt, I need to do some animal sacrifices to the cliff gods again. I’m still not sure if I believe in them, it’s a bit hit and miss, and also last time I made a sacrifice maybe I was too stingy with them, but a few times it did work for sure. Like, one time I did it, I went out and drove like a whole herd of animals over a cliff within one morning’s walk from the camp, got a few good guys together and brought them all back to the camp and dished animals out left and right. The women were very thankful.

I need to succeed. Please gods, please, please, please just send me one more cliff and soon.

I really want to share more meat with Tashi, I love seeing her eat meat. I used to not like such small women, when I was fatter during the cliff years, I was like “Give me the tallest woman, I don’t care, I’ll get her through the cold times, no biggy, I’ll come across some cliffs, don’t you worry babe, you just give me some tall sons.”

But these days, I’m attracted to small women, maybe because the fat years are over and the music is gonna stop. Or maybe it’s just that I’m really into Tashi and she isn’t all that tall. It’ll be trivial to share enough meat to get her through the cold times, even if I only bring back a few smaller animals, even if she carries my child, which I’m pretty sure she does. So small and sexy, yet she carries so much fat on her chest. She’s so receptive to me and seems to not care about all the other guys, downright hostile to the other guys. She’s great at getting berries and water too, I don’t know how she does it. Also, that woman knows how to make a fire, and is popular with the other women, which is, like, so very important. Great mother too, always keeping the children really close to her big chest, never heard any of them cry and attract wolves, not once. The way she dances around the fire too, it’s very different to the way other women dance. It really makes me want to dance like her and she always laughs when I try to imitate her dancing. We’ve got a real connection. Like, if she gives me any boys, they’d be really really good hunters I think. I’m maybe too old for them to protect me later and see them grow up becoming great hunters, but you never know. Because the boys who are yours, they do watch out for you more and share more meat with you and the girls that are yours share more berries with you, you can almost tell they’re yours by how much they share with you. And children don’t need that much food anyway, they are so small, it’s almost a joke how little they need and when they grow they might need some more food but then they also do start finding berries and catching small animals, so they need less than you think. It’s also easy to tell which boys are obsessed with bow and arrow from a young age, they want to know everything about your weapons, and they play with their own toy weapons acting as if they could already hunt down big game, they do crack me up, they really do, I’m always thinking ‘here have some meat little man, at this rate, you’ll be the best hunter in the clan in a few years’. It doesn’t even matter if they’re yours, because by the time they come of age, these youngsters are the most agile hunters, have more meat than they know what to do with, and remember you fondly and will share meat with you just to show off how manly they are.

Tashi really looks out for me. She’s less transactional than the others, because she can see into the future further, she can tell who has potential, she sees my potential, even if I’m not successful on the hunt one day, that doesn’t affect how many berries she shares. Her skill is that she’s not only looking at what is happening now, but she looks ahead in time, maybe more than anyone else I’ve ever met. She also tracks what’s going on in the group and gives you the relevant information of any risks, be they about the group or about matters of food stocks.

I think she knows that I’ll always try to protect her, like that time when that guy was trying to take her friend’s pelt, and she tried to help, and I speared that skinny motherfucker right in the back right there and then without hesitation, which was perhaps ill-advised, because his brothers were in my face right away, but I just gave them an ice cold stare and was like ‘back the fuck off, we’re not taking each other’s pelts in this clan, we’re not animals, come at me, and though I might die, I’ll be sure to take a couple of you down with me’, and then everyone agreed with me, because everyone was lowkey pissed off by these guys’ general behavior anyway (especially that skinny motherfucker had it coming). And then they did back off, and everyone looked at me with respect. And the way she looked at me afterwards, I think that was literally the best moment of my life. Like, you gotta have some rules, it’s what separates us from the animals. And Tashi and I agree on all the rules. And she’s so fierce too, like I think she’d fight a wolf if it was to protect her children. God I love that woman, I hope I get to see her again if and when I get back to camp, and she’s not away on menstrual seclusion.

Anyway, maybe that’s enough rest, maybe best to get back to it and take down those stupid animals. Gotta mobilize the crew, rally the troops, I need to do some ass kicking and get people hyped up for the hunt… you gotta do that even in bleak times, that’s the hallmark of a true leader. Gotta keep moving.
