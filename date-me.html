My failings, my failures: the usual topic. The failure of my life, of my thought. The failure of my writing. Familiar topics. My past failures, my present ones: yes, I could tell you about those. But my future failings … what about those? Where will I have gone wrong? What will I have done? What crimes have I yet to commit? How will I have managed to have failed anew? It’s quite a tense, isn’t it, the future perfect? Who will I have disappointed? You, probably. Whose hopes will I have defiled? Yours, of course. Your hopes. Ah, what will I have done to you in the future? What terrors await you? - Lars Iyer

Okay funny man, why not write about something serious for once. Enough already with the jestergooning. What’s with the witzelsucht? I don’t know. Witzelsucht actually is a thing… like it’s an actual neurological disorder. People making compulsive puns. It’s debilitating, often comorbid with dementia. Kind of interesting to think about that probably just like a dozen relatively simple systems and their permutations explain all the variance in human personalities… Maybe there should be like a personality test that just scores you on all the spectrum disorders… That really carves reality at its joints more. Like:

  • OCD. I’ve got that a bit, I do a fair amount of hand washing. Can be induced too psychopharmacologically too. The meth head cleaning their bathroom with the toothbrush. Or you’re coming up on the ‘vanse and just doing smalls and cleaning out your inbox or over editing your writing. Makes you think. I don’t like touching things on public transport. I read once they contain a lot of fecal matter and the toxoplasmosis in it is why people who move to cities disproportionately suffer from shizophrenia. There’s also some evidence that you can reduce psychotic symptoms via antibiotics. More suggestive evidence: My ex was really into cats and she too was a bit cray. Cray for leaving me.
  • ‘spie. We all know about that one. Who doesn’t forget to make the ol’ eye contact. Why am I not better at math though is anyone’s guess.
  • Utilization behavior (“a neurological phenomenon where a person compulsively and automatically grabs and uses objects in their visual field, even if the action is contextually inappropriate”). From a case study: “An examiner placed objects such as a tissue box, toothpaste and a toothbrush in front of the patient and before any instruction the patient brushed his teeth and picked up the tissue in a manner as if to blow his nose. When asked why he did these actions, he had no reason other than that he wanted to use the objects.” I’ve got that. We all have that though these days. “Oh look-ey some nicotine lozenges sitting on my desk. Might as well have one.” “Oh look-ey there’s the phone, let me just check that.” Phones are such a super stimulus though… we’re just not made for it. The mammalian visual system has evolved to catch bugs at night and find berries, and then you got all these colorful icons and apps that promise the world. No shame in utilizing them.
  • Relatedly: “Imitation behavior explains a patient's replication of the examiner's movements. This may be seen if, for example, the experimenter claps their hands and yawns, the patient would do the same in the same order.” I’ve got both a bit too. I think we all do. Monkey see, monkey do. I love aping people, especially the ones I like, it’s very tempting. Like, I once watched 10 seasons of curb back-to-back and transformed into literally larry david for a few months. Many such cases. Nobody must notice of course that’d be embarrassing. If you must adapt prose and poems, the words you use should be your own, like, don't plagiarise or take on loan. Cause there's always someone, somewhere, with a big nose, who knows, and who trips you up and laughs when you fall, who'll trip you up and laugh when you fall. I guess everyone’s doing it though and as you get older you just adopt a thing from this acquaintance and that celebrity and become a bland blend of those that have the most obnoxious and starkest memetic propagation and then you just hope nobody can decipher your ‘personality’. Preeeetty, preeetty, pretty good.
  • Parkinson’s / ADHD: there are apparently two dopamine systems in the brain, one for reacting to external stimuli and one to ‘self-initiate’. Like Parkinson’s patients can often catch balls or even run out of burning nursing homes only to then immediately be catatonic unable to initiate actions. That’s what happens with people with ADHD to an extent I think where some people make brilliant emergency room physicians or CEOs and are really good at putting out fires but make bad researchers because they can’t continuously self-initiate  long horizon research tasks, even if they’re interesting. I don’t know why I became a researcher, I’m really ill-suited to this job. I have shiny new object syndrome and start too many google docs. It helps to have an accountability system where you have to write like 500 words a day. But even then I’m always really close to the deadline. Should have become a journalist, but then journalism is dead now as we all will be because someone will build it and then everyone dies. So yeah anyway, do you date bloggers?
