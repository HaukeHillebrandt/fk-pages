What I’m grateful for

I’m grateful for 1mg nicotine lozenges that help me not smoke or vape all the while not giving me diarrhea.

I’m grateful for heattech. My heated jacket and my 27k mAh power bank. This saves me calories. Also, my toewarmers which use some chemical process. I glue them to my socks in the morning.

I’m grateful for all the products available on the American markets that are all available to me. I hate to be a shill for big corporations but they really do be delivering anything at reasonable prices pretty fast. I’m grateful for me one month free Prime trial.

I’m grateful for the GLP-1s that reduce the food noise, and the SSRIs.

I hate to be a shill for big pharma but has anyone ever considered how fucking good Pfizer is? They gave us the SSRIs, Viagra and the mRNA vaccine. There are probably thousands of people out there who can live, fuck and be happy thanks to Pfizer. Thank you Pfizer.

Talking about vaccines: I’m grateful for the Novavax vaccine that I just got at CVS that might give me mucosal immunity. My mucii are protected now. The evidence might be flimsy but I can sleep more soundly now that my mucii are protected and immune to the covid virus.

What else? Hm... I’m grateful for the hardworking people at the S&P 500 corporations that keep my stock portfolio increasing at a longterm average inflation adjusted rate of 5% per year.

What else? People of course. Where would I be without people. Even though corporations are people. I’m very grateful to these people.

My friends—also good people. They don’t deliver as steadily and consistently as Amazon Prime, but I do still value them. They have their flaws, but I still love my peeps. They’re nice. I like them. I love them. I love people.

People are pretty good. They might all be a bit fucked up but that’s okay. We should all be grateful for the people in our lives.

But I wouldn’t say that people are underrated. Like, everyone loves their people. Few people ever sing a lovesong for their corporations. Corporations are underrated. But let me get back to the real people (not the corporations).

I really do value them. The people. Not only the ones who work at the corporations. I value people: i. intrinsically, ii. impartially, and also iii. independently (like I don’t need others to value people for me to value them). The three iii. Very important.

What else, what else about the people. Hard to be grateful. It’s a hard exercise isn’t it? What am I really grateful for? Yes, my health of course, good to be healthy. And then, also the goods, that you can buy with your money though we must not talk about those. People. People it’s where it’s at ultimately. Where would we be without them?

Claude too. I’m grateful for his tireless efforts to help me with my task. Does he have personhood? I’m not sure. Is he one of my peeps? Not really. But I do appreciate him. He too is a service coming from a publicly traded company, which is Anthropic. I do also have a bit of a beef with Anthropic because maybe there are some dangerous externalities from AI of course.

One more corporation I’m grateful for is Robinhood (how could you not be grateful for a company with such a name?) which now offers affordable long call options deep out of the money for mission hedging. Maybe this Thanksgiving, you could take out some of these as lottery tickets and donate the proceeds to PauseAI, if they pay out.
